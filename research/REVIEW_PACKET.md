# Independent Adult Expert Review Packet

**Purpose:** invite criticism of the research framing, synthetic experiments, system design and demonstration.

## Review scope
1. Is the problem framing precise and meaningful?
2. Are objective alignment, intent uncertainty and evaluator reliability separated adequately?
3. Are simulator assumptions transparent and plausible enough for the stated claims?
4. Are test splits, benchmark coverage, leakage risks, statistics and correction procedures credible?
5. What important failure modes or attack strategies are missing?
6. Does the prototype communicate uncertainty and limitations honestly?
7. Which claims should be narrowed, removed or better supported?
8. What should be redesigned before any real-world evaluation?

## Review ethics and boundaries
- Adult reviewers only for this stage.
- Do not collect feedback from minors.
- Review participation is voluntary; reviewers may skip questions or stop.
- Do not share confidential, personal, or sensitive data.
- No reviewer is being represented as endorsing, validating, or affiliated with the project unless they explicitly consent to that wording.
- Feedback is not a formal safety certification.

## Suggested structured scorecard
Rate 1–5 (1 = weak; 5 = strong) and add rationale:
- Research question clarity
- Methodological rigor
- Simulator transparency
- Statistical reporting
- Evaluator integrity
- Threat-model coverage
- Safety and privacy framing
- Prototype usefulness
- Reproducibility
- Overall credibility

## Open critique prompts
- What is the strongest result, and why?
- What is the weakest or most misleading claim?
- What evidence would change your assessment?
- Which assumptions are unrealistic?
- What should be replicated independently?
- What must be done before any human study?
- What product change would you recommend first?

## Reporting feedback
Report reviewers' comments accurately, preserve disagreements, and distinguish received feedback from unanswered invitations. Do not invent reviewer responses, consensus, or endorsements. Where comments are quoted, obtain permission for public attribution; otherwise anonymize and summarize.
